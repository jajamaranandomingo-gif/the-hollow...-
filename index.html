THE HOLLOW: THE CHIME BEFORE THE SILENCE

By Unknown Archives Investigation Team
CLASSIFIED REPORT | LOCATION UNKNOWN
LAST UPDATED: NOVEMBER 2026
Figure not confirmed. An atmospheric illustration of the reported surroundings.

An unsettling mystery has spread throughout a quiet community following several reports of an unidentified individual known only as The Hollow. Descriptions of the figure have remained strangely consistent, despite the lack of evidence confirming its identity.

Residents describe an unusually dark silhouette, often appearing in places where almost no one is present. Some claim that the individual simply stands still, watching from a distance before disappearing without explanation.

WARNING: THE FOLLOWING ARTICLE CONTAINS FICTIONAL PARANORMAL ACCOUNTS AND UNVERIFIED WITNESS STORIES.

01 — THE APPEARANCE

Witnesses describe The Hollow as a tall, human-like silhouette with an entirely black appearance. Its body seems to blend into the darkness, making its actual shape difficult to distinguish.

The most recognizable feature is its pale, distorted scream mask. The mask has hollow black eyes and an unnaturally elongated expression, resembling a face permanently frozen in terror.

Several fictional witness accounts describe the figure as having an unusual stillness. It does not appear to move like an ordinary person, and its presence is often noticed only after someone looks in its direction.

02 — THE SOUND OF THE WIND CHIME

Perhaps the strangest detail surrounding The Hollow is a sound repeatedly mentioned in fictional witness reports: the gentle ringing of a wind chime.

According to these accounts, the sound appears unexpectedly, even when there is no visible wind or nearby object that could explain it.

Some residents believe that the ringing is a warning. They claim that whenever the chime is heard, The Hollow may already be nearby, and the person hearing it could be approaching a dangerous situation.

The sound is described as delicate and almost beautiful at first. However, its unusual timing has made it one of the most unsettling parts of the legend.

"It sounded like a little bell coming from somewhere outside. There was no wind. Then I noticed a figure standing in the distance."

— Anonymous fictional witness

03 — REPORTED SIGHTINGS

The first fictional account places the appearance of The Hollow near an abandoned structure outside the community. A resident reportedly noticed a dark figure standing beneath a broken streetlight.

Another account describes a similar silhouette appearing at the end of an empty road. The witness claimed to have heard a faint ringing before noticing the figure.

Other stories mention strange ringing sounds near windows, quiet streets, and abandoned buildings. Although the details vary, the same two elements continue to appear: the black silhouette and the distant sound of a wind chime.

04 — THE PATTERN

What makes the legend particularly disturbing is the supposed connection between the ringing and the appearance of the individual.

The wind chime is said to be heard first. Then comes an unusual silence, followed by reports of a motionless figure somewhere nearby.

No explanation has been established within the story. Some fictional investigators have suggested that the sound is simply a recurring detail in the accounts, while others believe it is part of The Hollow's mysterious presence.

05 — WHAT IS THE HOLLOW?

Despite the growing number of stories, the identity of The Hollow remains unknown. No reliable explanation has been established for its appearance, its mask, or the unusual ringing sound associated with it.

Is The Hollow simply an urban legend created by fear and imagination? Is the wind chime merely a coincidence repeated throughout the stories? Or is there something about the figure that nobody has managed to explain?
